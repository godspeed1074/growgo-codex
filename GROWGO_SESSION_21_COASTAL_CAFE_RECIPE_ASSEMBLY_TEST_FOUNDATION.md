## 3. Design Direction

The cafe should read as a bright, welcoming street-front building that fits the GrowGo Papercut 2.5D coastal visual direction.

### Required design qualities

- coastal
- bright
- welcoming
- papercut 2.5D compatible
- lightweight mobile-first

### Building characteristics

- street-facing frontage
- large glazing
- cafe entrance
- outdoor seating or deck area
- signage location
- service counter frontage
- coastal landscaping

### Visual intent

Compared with the residential coastal-family assets, the cafe should feel:

- more public
- more open to the street
- more glazing-led
- more signage-aware
- more deck and seating focused

## 4. Recipe Definition

### Core recipe output

The recipe should assemble a compact commercial coastal building that can anchor:

- beach-adjacent village strips
- foreshore hospitality clusters
- bakery and cafe streets
- motel or resort support precincts

### Footprint

- width: `7m-10m`
- depth: `8m-12m`
- lot type: coastal main-street or seaside frontage lot

### Frontage orientation

- the primary facade must face the nearest road or pedestrian frontage
- the glazing and entry must sit on the primary frontage side
- the outdoor seating zone must front the active public edge
- signage must align with the main camera-facing facade zone

### Property relationship

- shallow front setback preferred
- side landscape buffer optional
- rear service depth optional
- outdoor seating may sit on a deck or path-linked frontage terrace

These modules should not be created in this session. They are requirement definitions only.

Requirement summary:

## 8. Socket and Assembly Requirements

### Primary socket requirements

- `SOCKET_ROAD_FRONTAGE`
- `SOCKET_PATH_ENTRY`
- `SOCKET_WINDOW_LARGE`
- `SOCKET_SIGN_FACE`
- `SOCKET_TOP_EDGE`
- `SOCKET_ROOF_EAVE`
- `SOCKET_LANDSCAPE_PATCH`

### Assembly rules

- main glazing must align to the road-facing facade
- sign location must sit in a camera-readable band
- deck or seating zone must connect to the path system
- landscape softening must not block the entrance read
- the first recipe version should avoid over-dependence on unique one-off facade parts

### Duplicate avoidance rules

- do not duplicate residential coastal modules where scale and silhouette already solve the need
- do not rename existing modules to create commercial-only duplicates
- only promote a new commercial module when the identity need cannot be met by existing shell, roof, path, deck, trim, or landscape modules

## 9. LOD Expectations

### `LOD0`

- glazing rhythm visible
- entry clearly legible
- deck or seating layout readable
- awning and sign geometry readable if later approved

### `LOD1`

- storefront silhouette preserved
- public frontage and entry remain obvious
- seating or deck mass retained
- landscape softening remains visible

### `LOD2`

- simple commercial silhouette
- clear roof and glazing colour blocks
- sign zone retained as a readable frontage mark
- deck and frontage activity reduced to a broad hospitality shape
