## 4. Building Definition

### Visual direction

The building should read as:

- coastal
- bright
- welcoming
- public-facing
- papercut 2.5D compatible
- lightweight and overview-readable

### Functional characteristics

- road-facing commercial frontage
- obvious customer entrance
- clearly readable service window or service counter frontage
- awning-led weather cover
- sign-led hospitality identity
- outdoor seating zone connected to the public edge
- soft coastal landscaping around the frontage or side edge

## 5. Recipe Parameters

### Footprint size

- width: `7m-10m`
- depth: `8m-12m`
- frontage lot type: coastal main street, village strip, or foreshore hospitality lot

### Frontage direction

- the primary facade must face the nearest road or active pedestrian edge
- the frontage must align with the preferred north-facing presentation logic used by the current GrowGo visual pipeline

### Road-facing side

- front edge of the lot is the public hospitality edge
- sign, awning, service window, and seating all prioritize this frontage

### Entrance location

- entrance should sit on the primary frontage
- preferred placement: slightly offset from center to allow service-window balance
- alternate compact placement: central entry for smaller lot widths

### Service window location

- service window should sit on the same road-facing facade
- preferred placement: adjacent to but not overlapping the main entrance
- must retain strong visual read from gameplay and overview views

### Seating area location

- seating should sit on the public-facing frontage terrace
- placement may connect to deck edge or path-linked frontage zone
- seating must not block entry clarity

### Awning placement

- awning attaches above the primary frontage band
- projection should cover entry and service frontage
- deeper variants may also cover the front seating edge

### Sign mounting location

- main sign should mount in the frontage sign band or awning-adjacent location
- side or hanging sign variants may attach at the leading edge of the facade

### Landscaping placement

- small planting zones should soften the frontage corners or side buffer
- core path access must remain unobstructed
- tree placement should sit off the immediate facade zone to avoid blocking the building read

## 9. LOD Plan

### `LOD_CLOSE`

Purpose:

- detailed cafe identity

Requirements:

- awning profile clearly readable
- sign shape and bracket read clearly
- service-window counter relationship visible
- seating cluster composition readable
- entry, path, and frontage hierarchy obvious

### `LOD_GAMEPLAY`

Purpose:

- player interaction view

Requirements:

- building remains clearly readable as a cafe or bakery POI
- entrance and service frontage remain distinct
- seating remains recognizable as public hospitality space
- sign and awning retain identity without excessive detail

### `LOD_MAP`

Purpose:

- simplified POI representation

Requirements:

- broad coastal cafe silhouette preserved
- sign zone and awning remain implied in simplified form
- seating and service frontage collapse into a simplified hospitality frontage mass
- landscape reduces to readable soft edge blocks
