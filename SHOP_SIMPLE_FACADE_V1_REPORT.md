# Commercial Simple façade V1

Status: **PASS** — isolated first-pass candidate, locked pending operator approval.

The façade uses registered reusable wall bays, pilasters, base, fascia, top cap, awning rail, swappable awning/sign assets, and the approved Door/Window at scale `1.0`. Immediate opening casings were not reproduced by the façade. The exterior planter remains a separate prop and was not added.

Real Steam Deck Blender 5.2.0 LTS produced the beauty, component-ID, wireframe, and five oblique/rear QA renders. Facade-only budget: 178 triangles, 148 vertices, 5 materials, 296,650 bytes of compressed façade-specific presentation art, and zero anonymous geometry.

No Simple Shop integration, approved Door/Window source modification, Atlas change, Golden Reference modification, or eucalyptus work occurred.
