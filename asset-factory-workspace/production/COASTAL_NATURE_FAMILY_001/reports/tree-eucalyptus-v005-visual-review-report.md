# TREE_EUCALYPTUS_001 v005 — Approved Concept Replication Review

Status: **PENDING_OPERATOR_REVIEW**

## Design authority

The supplied `TREE_EUCALYPTUS_001_v005` GrowGo concept board is the primary visual target. `v005` was not judged primarily against earlier Blender candidates.

## Successfully replicated

- Approximately 14 m tall pale eucalyptus with a clean elegant taper.
- Six-part planted root flare and broad graphic cream/tan/rust bark blocking.
- Seven visible non-radial limb systems with open sky gaps.
- Nine distinct top/middle/lower canopy groups with asymmetric placement.
- Genuine front/back crown depth and coherent front, oblique, side, and top silhouettes.
- Dark inner foliage, mid-green body, light sage layering, and restrained new-growth accents.
- Rounded simplified cluster bodies with a limited ring of reusable three-leaf lanceolate sprays.
- Mobile geometry within the approved hard ceilings and simple seven-material construction.

## Biggest remaining differences

- The Blender clusters remain smoother and more consolidated than the concept's richly layered leaf bundles.
- The concept shows more numerous visible leaves and softer foliage edges; the candidate uses fewer larger sprays for mobile readability.
- The approved concept's trunk forks curve and interweave more organically; the candidate's major limbs remain cleaner and somewhat straighter.
- Bark patches are broader and fewer than the concept's denser peeling-bark rhythm.
- Root flare is structurally stronger than earlier candidates but less sculpturally rounded than the painted concept.

No registration, promotion, publication, runtime activation, active-asset replacement, or Atlas integration has occurred.
