- Established one dominant upper umbrella plus substantial upper-left,
  upper-right, middle-left, middle-right, lower-left, interior, and lower-right
  masses.

  light upper/front crowns, and lower drooping cards.

  retaining the main forks and negative spaces between islands.
- Reused the existing four-type 512×512 foliage atlas unchanged.
- Re-authored the trunk path and limb radii for a chunkier, curved game-art form.
- Added cream sculptural fork swells and six asymmetric rounded root toes.
- Retained surface-integrated bark materials; detached bark geometry remains zero.
