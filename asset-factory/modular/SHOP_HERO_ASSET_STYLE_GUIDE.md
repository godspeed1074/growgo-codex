# GrowGo Simple Shop Hero Asset Style Guide

## What good looks like

The target is a stylized premium mobile 2.5D/papercut asset: a clean readable silhouette, layered construction, deliberate shadow breaks, controlled palette materials, and small details that remain visible from the locked gameplay camera. The fascia/sign, awning, window, entrance, and planter should establish a clear storefront hierarchy at a glance.

## What failed previously

Earlier builds were technically valid but read as simplified blockouts. Their wall, fascia, glazing, door, awning, and vegetation were too flat or generic; material slots were duplicated; and detail was added without enough reusable visual value.

## Production target

Build depth through reusable Layer A modules: cornice and sign recess, fabric folds and scallops, recessed glazing and sills, door hardware and panels, stepped foundation, and layered foliage. Prefer silhouette/depth changes and shared atlas materials over tiny hidden geometry. Every detail must be independently reusable, LOD-tagged, budgeted, and component-identifiable. Layer B recipes may only compose registered Layer A IDs.

Golden Reference comparison remains the primary visual gate. Technical validation never substitutes for operator visual approval.
